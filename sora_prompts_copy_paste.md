# SORA PROMPTS - CINEMATIC FORMAT
## On Point Media Group: The Origin - Season One

Based on OpenAI Sora 2 best practices. Each prompt uses proper cinematic structure.

**FORMAT KEY:**
- Scene description in natural prose (50-100 words)
- Camera/Cinematography specs
- Lighting & Mood
- Action beats
- Dialogue in separate block

---

## EPISODE 1: "THE ALGORITHM"

```
A lone figure works late into the night. @ct_events2026 sits at a curved desk in a sleek home office, six monitors casting blue light across his face. On one screen, a real estate transaction flashes "PURCHASE CONFIRMED - $2.3B" over an image of a massive glass skyscraper. On another, an AI algorithm scans thousands of faces, text reading "TRUST ANALYSIS." The algorithm stops. A woman's profile appears: MAYA SANTOS. He leans forward, intrigued.

Camera: Over-the-shoulder medium shot, slow push-in toward screens
Lens: 35mm, shallow depth of field
Lighting: Cool blue from monitors, no other light source
Mood: Mysterious, calculated, late-night intensity

Actions:
- Algorithm scanning faces rapidly
- Screen flashes "MATCH FOUND"
- @ct_events2026 leans in, studies the profile
- Nods slowly, satisfied

Dialogue:
- @ct_events2026 (V.O.): "The building. The plan. But none of it works without the right person."
- @ct_events2026 (V.O.): "Maya Santos... what's your story?"
- @ct_events2026 (V.O.): "That's when everything changed."

End Card: Black screen, white text fades in: "THE ORIGIN OF ON POINT MEDIA GROUP"
```

## EPISODE 2: "THE SCREEN"

```
A video call that feels slightly off. On the left side of a split screen, a polished female headhunter with sleek blonde hair speaks from a sterile office background—but something about her movements feels too perfect, too rehearsed. On the right, Maya sits at her apartment desk, morning light warming her face. She has wild dark curls, striking blue eyes, and the focused posture of someone who takes every opportunity seriously. She treats this like any recruiting call. She has no idea.

Camera: Split screen, both subjects in medium close-up
Lens: 50mm portrait lens feel
Lighting: LEFT - flat corporate lighting. RIGHT - warm natural window light
Mood: Professional surface, underlying tension

Actions:
- Headhunter speaks with measured precision
- Maya listens, professional but curious
- She pauses when the question turns unusual

Dialogue:
- Headhunter: "Ms. Santos. Our algorithm identified you from three million candidates. Impressive background."
- Maya: "Thank you. Which company is this for?"
- Headhunter: "That comes later. First—can you keep a secret?"

End Card: Black screen, white text fades in: "THE ORIGIN OF ON POINT MEDIA GROUP"

## EPISODE 3: "THE TEST"

```

# CHARACTER CONSISTENCY NOTES

**MAYA SANTOS** - Describe naturally, not as a list:
- "Wild dark curls and striking blue eyes"
- "A striking woman with fierce blue eyes"
- "Her curls frame a face of sharp intelligence"

**@ct_events2026** - Always use the Sora code, never describe appearance

**MARCUS** - "A commanding presence in a tailored suit, moves like security not a driver"
